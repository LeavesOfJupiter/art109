This isn't a welcome place, but you're free to stick around anyway. If you inspect everything, you might get an answer. Or you might get hurt. This page doesn't exist to console you, or your own art.


Jupiter is an interactive digital artist with a focus on short stories and digital illustration. They are inspired by pieces such as My Boyfriend Came Home From the War, Brandon, and the works of artists like Laurie Anderson and Nam Jun Paik. They are currently working to expand their profile to include both old and new pieces, including interactive short stories about dysphoria, the queer identity, and similar themes of unfamiliarity. This page aims to capture that sensation and highlights current and past pieces that they have worked on the most.
